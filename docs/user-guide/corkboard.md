# The corkboard

The corkboard shows the scenes of a folder as cards, so you can look at the structure of
a chapter instead of at its prose.

Open it from the **Corkboard** tab at the top of the center panel, after selecting a
folder in the binder.

## What a card shows

- The scene's **title**.
- Its **synopsis**, the short description you write in the [inspector](inspector.md). If
  there is none, the card tells you where to write one.
- Its **word count**.
- An **Open** button that loads the scene into the editor.

The cards are a live view of the project: titles, synopses, and counts update as you
work, and they always follow the order of the binder.

```{note}
The corkboard is for planning and for seeing a chapter at a glance. Reordering and
renaming still happen in the [binder](binder.md), which is the single owner of the
project's structure.
```

## A routine that works well

1. Write a synopsis for every scene as you create it — one sentence is enough, and the
   inspector is right there.
2. Switch to the corkboard to check the chapter's shape: if two cards say the same
   thing, the chapter has a problem.
3. Fix the order in the binder, and go back to the editor.

If the folder has no scenes, the corkboard says so and points you back to the binder.
